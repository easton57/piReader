Welcome to piReader!

Controls:
1 tap: next page / move down
2 taps: previous page / move up
Long press: open the selected file or folder; while reading, back to the list
3 taps: also opens; in a PDF, switches between reflowed text and page images
".." at the top of a folder goes up one level
Hold 3 seconds: shut down.
The PiSugar button only does 1 tap, 2 taps and long press.

Your place in every book is saved on each page turn, and the last book you had open reopens on boot.

Add books by copying them into the library folder or through the web UI on port 5000. Supported: .txt, .md, .epub and .pdf.
